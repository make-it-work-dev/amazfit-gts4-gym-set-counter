Gym Sets Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Gym Sets is a smartwatch application published by Gym Sets and developed by Paz Ohana.

Information stored on the watch

Gym Sets stores workout settings, active timer state, and workout history locally on the user's Amazfit watch. This information is used only to provide the app's set counter, rest timer, and history features. Gym Sets does not transmit workout history or body-part selections to the developer.

Users can remove individual history entries inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data, subject to the behavior of Zepp OS and the Zepp mobile application.

License and payment processing

Gym Sets uses KiezelPay to provide a $2.99 one-time lifetime license. To verify a license, the app and its companion Side Service may send KiezelPay a randomly generated account token, the Gym Sets product identifier, the device model or part number, platform information, installation time, and license-check information. KiezelPay processes the purchase and maintains the associated payment and license records.

Gym Sets does not receive or store the user's full payment-card information. Payment processing, transaction records, refunds, tax handling, and license restoration are governed by KiezelPay's own terms and privacy policy.

Data sharing

Information is shared with KiezelPay only as needed to create, verify, restore, and support the purchased license. Gym Sets does not sell workout data or use it for advertising.

Zepp Health may process information required to distribute and operate the app under its own terms and privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Gym Sets is a general fitness utility and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Paz@makeitwork.dev.
