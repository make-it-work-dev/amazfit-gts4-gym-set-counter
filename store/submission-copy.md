## Full description

Keep your workout moving without reaching for your phone.

Gym Sets provides a clear, adjustable set counter built for the Amazfit GTS 4. Tap once when a set is complete and the app starts an adjustable rest countdown. A strong two-second wrist reminder tells you when it is time for the next set.

Features:

- Adjustable 1–20 set workout target
- Adjustable 15-second to 10-minute rest duration
- Rest timer that survives screen-off and app switching
- Strong vibration reminder when rest ends
- Workout history organized by date and body part
- Manual history editing and backdated workout entry
- Quick launch of the watch's native phone-music controls
- Local storage of workout settings and history

Supported devices:

- Amazfit GTS 4
- Amazfit GTS 4-W

## Payment disclosure

Gym Sets requires a **$2.99 one-time lifetime license** after installation. Payment and license restoration are provided through KiezelPay. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal.

## Permissions explanation

- **Local storage:** Saves timer state, settings, and workout history on the watch.
- **Device information:** Supplies the watch model to KiezelPay for license verification.
- **Internet / Side Service:** Allows the Zepp mobile app's companion service to verify and restore the KiezelPay license.

Gym Sets does not upload workout history or body-part selections.
